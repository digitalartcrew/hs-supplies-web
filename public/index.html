05 / Questions

Before You Come In

Where is Hard Hitta Supplies located?

We're at 165 Mississippi Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, in Potrero Hill. Street parking is available and we're a short walk from the 22nd Street Caltrain station.

What are your dispensary hours?

Seven days a week, 9:00am to 9:45pm Pacific — weekends and most holidays included.

Do you deliver cannabis in San Francisco?

Yes. We deliver across San Francisco, with a typical window of about 60 minutes. Order from the live menu and have a valid government-issued ID ready at the door.

Do I need a medical card to shop here?

No. We serve adult-use customers 21 and over with a valid government-issued ID. Patients 18 and over may shop with a valid California medical recommendation.

Are you a licensed dispensary?

Yes — California cannabis retail license C10-0000768-LIC. Every product on our shelves is lab-tested and traceable from cultivator to counter.

Can I order online for pickup?

Yes. Browse the live menu, place an express pickup order, and collect it in store. Bring the ID you ordered with.
